Overview

FDE ("we," "us," or "our") provides personalized engineering assessment and mentorship. This policy explains what information we collect when you apply for an assessment or otherwise interact with this site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By submitting the application form or otherwise providing information to us, you agree to the practices described here. If you don't agree, please don't submit your information.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, primarily through the assessment application form:

  • Contact details — full name, email address, phone number, country
  • Background information — current education, current job, years of experience, programming languages, AI experience
  • Goals and context — skill level, career goal, dream company, biggest challenge, three-year goals, weekly availability, preferred learning language

We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as browser type, device type, and general usage patterns on this site, for the purposes described below.

How we use it

We use the information you provide to:

  • Evaluate your application and prepare your personalized roadmap
  • Determine an estimated timeline and pricing specific to your goals
  • Communicate with you about your assessment, mentorship, and account
  • Improve how we assess applicants and structure roadmaps
  • Meet legal, security, and operational obligations

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects without human review.

Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in limited circumstances:

  • With service providers who help us operate — for example, email delivery or scheduling tools — under obligations to protect your data
  • If required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of FDE, our learners, or others
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you where required

Data retention

We retain application information for as long as necessary to evaluate your application, provide mentorship if you proceed, and meet legal or accounting obligations. If you don't proceed past the assessment stage, we retain your application for a limited period before deleting or anonymizing it, unless you ask us to remove it sooner.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Cookies & analytics

This site may use essential cookies required for basic functionality, and limited analytics to understand how the site is used, in aggregate. We do not use cookies to serve ads, and we don't share browsing data with advertisers.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to limit access to your information to those who need it.

Children's privacy

This program is not directed at children under 16. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has submitted information to us, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices evolve. If we make material changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above. Continued use of this site after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
